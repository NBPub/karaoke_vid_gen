What's on screen

Every element below is drawn by the renderer and is configurable (Configuration → [render]):

  • Per-word fill: each letter sweeps to fill_color as it's sung; already-sung lines sit in past_color.
  • Song progress bar: a persistent bar down the right edge, filling across the track.
  • Title card: artist / title over the opening, with a lead-in so singing never starts before there's time to read.
  • Count-in dots: a 3-2-1 before the first line and after long gaps.
  • Wait bar: a filling bar over long instrumental breaks.
  • Background-vocal words: backing parts allowed to overlap the lead.
  • Line splitting: lines too wide for the frame wrap into a left-justified block (below).

Count-in dots before "Code Monkey have every reason."

Line splitting

A line too wide for the frame is flagged by the preflight check, then fit by karaoke split: balanced into the fewest rows that fit and rendered as a left-justified block.
